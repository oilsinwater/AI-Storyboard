The output contains only the prompts. No metadata block, no notes, no instructions.

Prompt structure:
[Simplified subject] [primary action] [direction] [+ camera movement]. [Pacing]. [Duration: X seconds].

Rules: 40-80 words; one primary action; direction explicit; pace stated; camera named (pan, tilt, dolly, truck, zoom, orbit, handheld, static); duration 3-5 seconds and realistic for the action; key identifiers and frame positions inherited from the panel; consecutive prompts chain so the end of one is the start of the next.
