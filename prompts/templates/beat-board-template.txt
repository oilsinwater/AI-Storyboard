Beat 1: The Torn Fine
Visual Description: Medium shot, eye-level. A 25-year-old woman with waist-length straight silver hair, pale skin, violet eyes, wearing a dark red wool coat over a grey turtleneck sits behind a wooden returns desk in a cavernous, empty library reading room, tearing a printed fine slip in half while a small boy in a yellow raincoat watches from across the desk, eyes down. A cart of returned books stands beside her; high windows throw long shafts of light across empty oak tables behind them. Her face stays flat and unsmiling as the two halves drop toward a wastebasket. Digital concept art, painterly style, detailed environment.
Lighting & Mood: Cool grey morning light falls in hard diagonal shafts from the tall windows, catching dust in the air and leaving the far shelves in shadow. Quiet, hushed, institutional calm with a small private act of mercy at its center.
